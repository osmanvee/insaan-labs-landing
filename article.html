humans and AI

AI is no longer a future idea. It is very much here. Artists, Engineers, Accountants and even Doctors are already relying on it to make decisions.

Alignment is not about slowing progress down. It is about making sure progress goes somewhere worth going. Human context is important for systems. Many systems are prioritizing the wrong things: speed over care, automation over agency.

Humans bring intent, values, and judgment. AI brings scale, pattern recognition, and execution.

Insaan (انسان) means human in Urdu and Arabic — which is why it is in the name.
